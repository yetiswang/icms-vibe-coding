Act 2 · Scene 5

A harness, in three legs.

🛠
Tools
Small helpers Claude can call: search the web, open and edit a file, run a saved step, look something up online.
Claude picks which helper to use, and how, depending on what you asked for.
📁
Files
It reads and writes the real files on your laptop. Your folders, your notes, your images.
Background becomes everything you have already written. The output is a real saved file you can open later.
🔁
Loops
It runs a step, reads what came back, decides what to do next, runs again.
Something doesn't work? It reads the error message and tries a different way.

Tools + Files + Loops = an agent that can actually finish the task,
not just describe how you'd do it.

Act 3 · Scene 8

The workflow, in three moves.

01
Read
Point Claude at your existing assets.
Templates, slides, notes, screenshots.
It learns your visual style, vocabulary, and audience by reading what you've already made. No "design system" doc needed.
02
Repo
Make a place online to keep the work.
Tracked. Shareable. One click to publish.
Every change is a snapshot you can return to. The site goes live with a button. No design tools, no upload step.
03
Vibe
Describe what you want, in plain words.
Watch it appear. Ask for changes.
"Make the title bigger." "Use the supramolecular image." "Add a section for collaborators." It edits, refreshes, you react.
