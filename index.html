Act 4 · Scene 13

Two paths to get started.

Subscription path
Hosted, fastest models
  • Claude Pro / Max
  • ChatGPT Plus
  • Cursor Pro
€20–200 / month
No setup. Best models. Just sign up.
Open-source path
Private, free, your hardware
  • Ollama · LM Studio
  • Continue.dev (VS Code)
  • Aider · llama.cpp
€0 / month
Runs on a Mac or workstation. Slower, smaller, yours.

Both paths get you there. Pick what fits your ethics, budget, and hardware.
